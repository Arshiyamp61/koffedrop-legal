KoffeDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026  |  Effective date: August 22, 2026

KoffeDrop ("the extension", "the Service", "we", "us", "our") is a Chrome extension that captures your AI conversation context (from supported platforms such as Claude.ai and ChatGPT) and generates a compressed handoff summary you can transfer to another AI session using a one-time PIN. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, who we share it with, and what choices and rights you have.

By installing or using KoffeDrop, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the extension.

1. Who we are

KoffeDrop is developed and operated independently by KoffeDrop. We are the "data controller" for the personal information described in this policy, meaning we decide how and why your information is processed. For any privacy-related question, request, or concern, you can reach us at the email address listed in the Contact section below.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate the Service. We do not collect your general browsing history, and we do not track you across websites unrelated to KoffeDrop's function.

3. How we collect information

Capture only happens when you take an explicit action — clicking the "Capture" button on a supported AI chat page. The extension does not silently monitor or record your activity in the background, and it does not run on pages other than the supported AI chat platforms.

4. Why we use your information

  • To provide the core Service — turning your captured conversation into a compressed handoff summary, and letting you retrieve it elsewhere with a PIN.
  • To authenticate you — verifying your identity via magic-link email sign-in so that only you can access your capsules.
  • To secure the Service — detecting and rate-limiting abusive PIN-guessing attempts, and preventing accidental overuse of shared processing capacity.
  • To maintain and improve the Service — using anonymized or aggregated usage data to identify bugs, reliability issues, and areas for improvement.
  • To communicate with you — for example, sending you the magic-link sign-in email, or responding to a support request you initiate.

We do not use your captured conversation content for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles from your data.

If KoffeDrop is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we will notify you of any such change and any resulting choices you may have.

7. How we protect your information

We apply industry-standard safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we handle, including access controls that restrict data to your own account (row-level security), encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, and time-limited storage so captured content does not linger longer than necessary. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to apply reasonable and appropriate measures and to improve them over time.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — request that we correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and associated data.
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the Service and sign out at any time; you can also delete individual capsules directly within the extension.
  • Object or restrict — object to certain processing, where applicable law provides this right.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in the Contact section. We will respond to verified requests within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity (for example, by confirming control of the email address on your account) before acting on a request.

9. Content involving other people

If your captured AI conversation contains personal information about a third party — for example, details about a client, colleague, or family member that you pasted into the chat — capturing it will send that information to the third-party providers listed in Section 5. Please avoid capturing conversations that contain other people's sensitive or personal information unless you have the right to share it.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

The extension itself does not use tracking cookies. Our authentication provider (Supabase) may use session tokens stored in your browser's local extension storage (not regular browser cookies) to keep you signed in. This data stays on your device and is used only to maintain your session.

11. International users

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required by applicable law, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms offered by our providers) for such transfers.

12. Children's privacy

KoffeDrop is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under the applicable minimum age, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

13. Browser permissions we request

KoffeDrop requests certain Chrome extension permissions solely to operate its core function:

  • Access to supported AI chat pages — to read the visible conversation content when you click Capture, and to inject a retrieved summary when you click DROP.
  • Storage — to keep you signed in and to temporarily hold capture progress on your device.

We request the minimum permissions necessary and do not request broad access to all websites you visit.

14. Third-party services and links

This policy does not cover the privacy practices of the AI platforms you use (such as Claude.ai or ChatGPT) or any other third-party website. Please review those services' own privacy policies separately.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. If we make a material change, we will notify you via an update notice within the extension and by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of KoffeDrop after a material change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy; for material changes, you will also be shown an updated consent screen the next time you open the extension.
